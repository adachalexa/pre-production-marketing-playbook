Did it work?

The result

Domestic total / opening weekend $263.5M / $17.2M Made $17.2M in its first weekend in the US and Canada and $263.5M there by the end of its run, plus $521.3M worldwide. #4 of 6
Return on cost $311.1M Estimated profit after all costs. Focus never reported its marketing spend, so this comes from a profit model rather than a simple return figure. Model estimate
Beat the pre-release forecast 1.72× Opened at $17.2M, 1.7 times what was expected. Tracking expected about $10M. #3 of 5
02

How was it received?

Critics vs. audience

Critic reviews 330 + 38 330 critics reviewed it on Rotten Tomatoes and 38 on Metacritic. More reviews means more press attention.
Review scores 93% · 77 93% of critics gave it a positive review and they averaged 77/100 on Metacritic. Opening-night audiences graded it A- (CinemaScore). #4 of 6
Critics vs. regular viewers 1-pt gap Critics scored it 77/100; everyday Metacritic users gave it 78/100. On Rotten Tomatoes, 93% of critics and 94% of audiences liked it. Critics and audiences mostly agreed.
03

What was made

Production pull and budget efficiency

Production pull (score, cast, sets) $750K · 20 days Made for $750,000 in 20 days by a YouTube creator, built around one prop (the wish-granting One Wish Willow) and one unforgettable look (Nikki's eyes and makeup).
Budget efficiency 695.0× Earned 695.0 times its $750K production budget in worldwide ticket sales (marketing costs not included). #1 of 6
Why it mattered and who it reached Sold the film, fed TikTok Doing so much with $750K made it an underdog story: Focus bought it for about $15M, 20 times its budget, before a ticket was sold. Its prop and makeup became TikTok trends for young viewers, though only 9% of audience posts mentioned the filmmaking; they talked about the story.
04

Who was in it and who they reached

Cast reach, amplification, pairing and fans

Cast reach Unknown lead Star Inde Navarrette was a newcomer. Posts about the cast jumped from 146 to 1,254 after release as her character, Nikki, broke out.
Cast amplification share 53% Of 4,942 posts on X that named the cast or the director, 53% named the cast. Higher means the stars, not the filmmaker, drove the conversation.
Pairing lift Not measured Not tracked for this film. Only The Odyssey and Anyone But You have data on stars appearing together.
Fan participation 400K 400,000 fans signed up to get text messages from Nikki, 1.7M played an online quest, and one fan's copy of Nikki's makeup got 6.4M views.
05

How it was sold

Buzz, trust and premium formats

Scarcity = buzz (first reaction) Sep 2025 · 8 months out First shown at the Toronto film festival's midnight lineup, then at sold-out festival screenings, so buzz built for months before release.
Audience trust (influencer marketing) 70% 70% of opening-weekend viewers said they would definitely recommend it. Fans, creators and college ambassadors saw it at early screenings before release. #3 of 5Screenings unverified
Premium format share 0% 0% of opening-weekend ticket sales came from IMAX and other premium screens. It played only on regular screens. #5 of 5Unverified
06

Who showed up

Attendance, drivers and demographics

Attendance rate $6,576 Each theater averaged this much in ticket sales on opening weekend, a sign of how full the screens were. #5 of 6
Attendance drivers Not polled No survey asked why. It beat its $10M forecast thanks to the director's YouTube fans, then word of mouth took over. Not polled
Demographics 78% under 35 59% male, and one of the youngest crowds of the six. About 60% were Black, Latino or Asian (one report). Partly unverified
Cast effects on demographics No-star youth With no famous stars, an unknown lead and a YouTube-creator director drew one of the youngest audiences.
07

What people said online

Volume, sentiment, virality and UGC

Conversation volume 2,332 Posts on X around its release, and 251 more in the last 90 days. Its top 50 Reddit posts drew 2,457 comments. #5 of 6
Audience sentiment 4.1★ Average rating out of 5 on Letterboxd, where 44% of users gave it 4.5 or 5 stars. 94% of Rotten Tomatoes audiences liked it. #3 of 6
Viral per platform 8.1M Plays on its single biggest TikTok. Fans made more than 652,000 #Obsession posts by late May. Its 100 latest TikToks drew 14,517,411 plays.
User-generated content (UGC) 652K+ #Obsession posts on TikTok as fans recreated Nikki's makeup, her creepy walk and the 'no, no, no' date scene.
08

Did it last?

Staying power

Best staying power 15.3× Over its full run in the US it earned 15.3 times its opening weekend. Its second weekend was up 39% from the first. It grew in its second and third weekends and led the box office on weekdays. #1 of 6
