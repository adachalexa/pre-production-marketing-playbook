Did it work?

The result

Domestic total / opening weekend $636.8M / $162.0M Made $162.0M in its first weekend in the US and Canada and $636.8M there by the end of its run, plus $1.45B worldwide. #1 of 6
Return on cost 4.9× Every $1 spent making and marketing it ($145.0M + $150.0M) brought back $4.91 in worldwide ticket sales. Estimated profit: about $547.9M. #2 of 5
Beat the pre-release forecast 2.16× Opened at $162.0M, 2.2 times what was expected. Warner Bros. projected $75M; outside tracking said $90M–$130M. #2 of 5
02

How was it received?

Critics vs. audience

Critic reviews 509 + 67 509 critics reviewed it on Rotten Tomatoes and 67 on Metacritic. More reviews means more press attention.
Review scores 88% · 80 88% of critics gave it a positive review and they averaged 80/100 on Metacritic. Opening-night audiences graded it A (CinemaScore). #3 of 6
Critics vs. regular viewers 22-pt gap Critics scored it 80/100; everyday Metacritic users gave it 58/100. On Rotten Tomatoes, 88% of critics and 83% of audiences liked it. Critics liked it far more than regular viewers. Its earliest fans on Letterboxd were glowing: 4.55★, 97% positive.
03

What was made

Production pull and budget efficiency

Production pull (score, cast, sets) 4 craft Oscar noms A hand-built, hand-painted pink Barbie Land (the set used so much pink paint the supplier ran out), Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling, and songs that earned two Oscar nominations and one win.
Budget efficiency 10.0× Earned 10.0 times its $145.0M production budget in worldwide ticket sales (marketing costs not included). #3 of 6
Why it mattered and who it reached Made pre-release news The pink-paint shortage story ran 46 days before release and turned set design into headlines, drawing fashion and brand fans and the most female audience of the six (65%). After seeing it, people talked more about Ken and the themes; only 14% of posts mentioned the filmmaking.
04

Who was in it and who they reached

Cast reach, amplification, pairing and fans

Cast reach Robbie + Gosling Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling were mentioned about twice as often as director Greta Gerwig on X, and Robbie turned the press tour into a Barbie fashion show.
Cast amplification share 66% Of 735 posts on X that named the cast or the director, 66% named the cast. Higher means the stars, not the filmmaker, drove the conversation. Thin 2023 X archive
Pairing lift Not measured Not tracked for this film. Only The Odyssey and Anyone But You have data on stars appearing together.
Fan participation 200K+ More than 200,000 people booked same-day Barbie and Oppenheimer double features, and fans dressed in pink for screenings.
05

How it was sold

Buzz, trust and premium formats

Scarcity = buzz (first reaction) Jul 10, 2023 · 11 days out Influencers and preview audiences posted first. The second teaser alone drew 7.5M engagements in one day.
Audience trust (influencer marketing) 5/5 PostTrak Warner Bros. went influencer-first, with 244 influencer partnerships reaching 438M impressions. Opening audiences gave it 5 out of 5 stars in exit polls. No recommend %
Premium format share 10% 10% of opening-weekend ticket sales came from IMAX and other premium screens. Oppenheimer held about 80% of premium screens that weekend. #4 of 5Unverified
06

Who showed up

Attendance, drivers and demographics

Attendance rate $38,186 Each theater averaged this much in ticket sales on opening weekend, a sign of how full the screens were. About 12.8M people went opening weekend. #1 of 6
Attendance drivers Group outings No survey asked why, but buyers bought about 3 tickets at a time and 200,000+ booked Barbenheimer double features, a sign people came with friends. Not polled
Demographics 65% female 81% were under 35 and 40% under 25. The audience was 56% white, 23% Hispanic, 10% Black and 7% Asian.
Cast effects on demographics Star-led skew Margot Robbie and a fashion-led pink campaign pulled the most female audience of the six films (65%).
07

What people said online

Volume, sentiment, virality and UGC

Conversation volume 42,082 Comments on its 50 most popular Reddit posts (297,590 upvotes). Older X data from 2023 is too incomplete to compare. #1 of 6
Audience sentiment 3.7★ Average rating out of 5 on Letterboxd, where 28% of users gave it 4.5 or 5 stars. 83% of Rotten Tomatoes audiences liked it. #4 of 6
Viral per platform 4.6B Views on #BarbieTheMovie, with 23M likes, comments and shares, according to Mattel. Its 100 latest TikToks drew 5,657,894 plays. Company-reported
User-generated content (UGC) 13M People made their own Barbie movie posters with the Barbie Selfie Generator.
08

Did it last?

Staying power

Best staying power 3.9× Over its full run in the US it earned 3.9 times its opening weekend. Its second weekend was down 43% from the first. A one-week IMAX run in September brought its smallest late drop. #5 of 6
