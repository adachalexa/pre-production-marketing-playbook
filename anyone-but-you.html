Anyone But You

01

Did it work?

The result

Domestic total / opening weekend $88.3M / $6.0M Made $6.0M in its first weekend in the US and Canada and $88.3M there by the end of its run, plus $219.8M worldwide. #6 of 6
Return on cost 2.4× Every $1 spent making and marketing it ($25.0M + $65.0M) brought back $2.44 in worldwide ticket sales. Estimated profit: about $59.5M. #5 of 5
Beat the pre-release forecast Not collected No one published an opening prediction. Its $6M Christmas debut was called a disappointment, then the film kept growing for weeks.
02

How was it received?

Critics vs. audience

Critic reviews 118 + 26 118 critics reviewed it on Rotten Tomatoes and 26 on Metacritic. More reviews means more press attention.
Review scores 53% · 52 53% of critics gave it a positive review and they averaged 52/100 on Metacritic. Opening-night audiences graded it B+ (CinemaScore). #6 of 6
Critics vs. regular viewers 6-pt gap Critics scored it 52/100; everyday Metacritic users gave it 58/100. On Rotten Tomatoes, 53% of critics and 87% of audiences liked it. Critics and audiences mostly agreed.
03

What was made

Production pull and budget efficiency

Production pull (score, cast, sets) Sydney on location Filmed at real Sydney landmarks (the Opera House, Harbour Bridge and Bondi) with Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell, using the song 'Unwritten' as a recurring moment.
Budget efficiency 8.8× Earned 8.8 times its $25.0M production budget in worldwide ticket sales (marketing costs not included). #4 of 6
Why it mattered and who it reached Escapism + a song Sunny Sydney locations gave winter audiences an escapist getaway, and the 'Unwritten' scene gave young women a moment to recreate, sparking the exit-dance trend. The filmmaking itself got little attention (12% of posts).
04

Who was in it and who they reached

Cast reach, amplification, pairing and fans

Cast reach Sweeney +129% Sydney Sweeney's TikTok following more than doubled (up 129%) and Glen Powell's grew 76% during the campaign, according to Sony.
Cast amplification share 94% Of 35 posts on X that named the cast or the director, 94% named the cast. Higher means the stars, not the filmmaker, drove the conversation. Thin 2023 X archive
Pairing lift 1.8× A casual clip of Sweeney and Powell whispering together got 18M views, 1.8 times the official trailer's 10M. Clip vs. trailer
Fan participation 602M Views of TikToks showing audiences dancing to 'Unwritten' as they left the theater. The trend started with fans, and Sony joined in.
05

How it was sold

Buzz, trust and premium formats

Scarcity = buzz (first reaction) Dec 11, 2023 · 11 days out Critics and podcasters posted glowing first reactions on X about 11 days before release.
Audience trust (influencer marketing) 57% 57% of opening-weekend viewers said they would definitely recommend it. Sony paired with young influencers in every market for 'Get Ready with Me' style videos. #4 of 5
Premium format share Not found Released on regular screens; no premium share was reported.
06

Who showed up

Attendance, drivers and demographics

Attendance rate $1,964 Each theater averaged this much in ticket sales on opening weekend, a sign of how full the screens were. #6 of 6
Attendance drivers Not polled No survey asked why. Reports credit the TikTok trend and young women coming back for repeat viewings. Not polled
Demographics Women 14–40 No opening-weekend age breakdown was published. Young women drove the run, and 37% were 18–24 by its third weekend.
Cast effects on demographics Young women Sweeney and Powell's chemistry on and off screen drew young women, who came back with friends.

What people said online

Volume, sentiment, virality and UGC

Conversation volume 4,334 Comments on its 50 most popular Reddit posts (98,608 upvotes). Older X data from 2023 is too incomplete to compare. #4 of 6
Audience sentiment 2.8★ Average rating out of 5 on Letterboxd, where 10% of users gave it 4.5 or 5 stars. 87% of Rotten Tomatoes audiences liked it. #6 of 6
Viral per platform 2.7B Views on #AnyoneButYou on TikTok, and the song 'Unwritten' climbed back to No. 7 on TikTok's chart, according to Sony. Its 100 latest TikToks drew 12,153 plays. Company-reported

Did it last?

Staying power

Best staying power 14.7× Over its full run in the US it earned 14.7 times its opening weekend. Its second weekend was up 46% from the first. It grew four weeks in a row, then drew 24.7M views on Netflix. #2 of 6
